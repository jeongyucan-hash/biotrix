# Research icons and diagrams

The research icon sheet was generated and approved by the user in this session on 2026-10-02 (KST). The approved PNG is kept in the session; research-icons.webp is a format-converted copy, displayed as five individual icon regions through CSS. No original logo paths were edited.

The new inline SVG process icons and HTML diagrams describe intended research and development principles, the BIO + MATRIX name concept, and contact topics. They do not establish owned technology, clinical results or a product mechanism.

Diagram copy is available in English and Korean. English is the initial default, with an explicit visitor preference stored as biotrix-language in localStorage. This stores only a display preference. No analytics, accounts or tracking were added.
