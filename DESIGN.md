# BIOTRIX Brand System 1.1

2026-10-02. User-directed replacement of the former green / ivory system. This file supersedes the color and logo guidance in DESIGN_SYSTEM.md and HERO_DESIGN.md. Preserve those files as history and image provenance.

## Identity
Use the curved-fold BIO master: original ribbon B, 20-unit I, O sidewalls and TRIX vertical stems, and curved folds based on B's left stem. X retains its 82 × 100 envelope and has a 2.4-unit crossing separation and a 20-unit perpendicular diagonal thickness. Do not add dots, enclosing shapes, glow, arbitrary shadows or a second B emblem.

Assets: assets/biotrix-wordmark-{primary,reverse,mono,white}.svg. The wordmark is an outlined drawing, not a typeface. Preserve the 590:152 canvas ratio and its built-in clear space. Primary is for light surfaces; reverse is for navy surfaces.

## Typography and layout
Use a reliable sans stack: Arial, Noto Sans KR, Malgun Gothic, sans-serif. Logo lettering is independent. Desktop display max 64px; H2 max 48px; body 16px and line-height 1.85. Mobile display 32–46px; body 14–16px. Avoid serif/italic headings. Keep Korean words together.

1280px maximum content width, 20px mobile side gutters. Desktop spacing 64–96px; mobile 44–64px. Rectangular surfaces, thin rules, no rounded section containers. Home programs use compact rows; detailed program pages retain explanatory content. Header uses the whole wordmark, never a symbol plus a separately typed company name.

## Photography and motion
Existing conceptual images retain provenance and truthful alt text. Neutral monochrome treatment supports the silver palette; imagery does not imply actual facilities or clinical results. Carousel is manual-first with optional play, previous/next and pause controls. Respect reduced motion; no added parallax or decorative continuous animation.
