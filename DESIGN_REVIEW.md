# BIOTRIX design review and redesign — 2026-10-02

## Assessment
The previous site was orderly but visually generic: family/researcher stock-style imagery, repetitive long bilingual rows and box-arrow diagrams did not reveal a distinctive biological or commercial story. Grayscale intensified a somber mood. The five fields were legible as categories, but not visually intelligible as biological processes. Stronger imagery alone cannot substantiate an actual technology or differentiating capability.

## Implemented direction
All three people-based hero photographs removed from the page. New bright, original cell/material concept visualization provides a full-width scientific cover, with the approved message retained. Navy/silver CI preserved with warm ivory and restrained coral/blue biological accents.

Added a five-field mechanism explorer (keyboard-native buttons, explicit selected state, no automatic motion; all content available without JavaScript). Added five deterministic, editable biological diagrams to Science and Programs, replacing generic process boxes. These explain nutrients/mitochondria/ATP; microbial products/epithelial interface; metabolic state/immune response; fibroblast/extracellular matrix; fermentation/separation/characterization. Captions distinguish biological background and evaluation from proven product efficacy. The new B favicon/cache version remains included from the pending previous correction.

## Scientific framing
Diagrams are simplified biological-context illustrations, not BIOTRIX experimental data. FLORA non-live material is not equated with probiotic clinical efficacy. IMMERA does not claim universal immune enhancement; RENOVA does not claim aging reversal. No delivery or molecular target capability is invented. Hero is conceptual, not microscopy.

Background primary studies: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08629-4 (macrophage nutrient recycling and immunometabolism); https://www.nature.com/articles/s42255-018-0008-5 (fibroblast metabolism and extracellular matrix); https://www.nature.com/articles/s41514-020-0042-x (senescent fibroblasts in organotypic skin models). Context only, not evidence for these BIOTRIX materials.

## Remaining limitations
Original generated hero is artistic and not an anatomically exact teaching model. Diagrams prioritize readable systems over detailed pathways. Actual material composition, target validation and performance data must guide any future product-specific mechanism claims. Five program names are positioning, not established trademark clearance. Production release requires approval.
