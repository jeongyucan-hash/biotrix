# BIOTRIX website audit — 2026-10-03

Reviewed the live Home, Company, Science, Programs, Contact, Privacy, Terms and three legacy routes in Microsoft Edge. Compared official Roche, Novartis and Recursion pages for narrative and information architecture. The earlier benchmark report was not available in this checkout; this is a fresh comparison.

## Findings and changes

| Finding | Response |
|---|---|
| Research icons overlapped names; mobile names overlapped descriptions | Scoped marker/content grid rules and explicit mobile row placement |
| Company mission and founder statement narrowed the vision to ingredient supply | Reframed around unmet need, AI-assisted discovery, validation, production and access |
| AI appeared deep in Science as a present-tense operational claim | Added to hero/principles and described intended approach rather than a validated platform |
| Five named programs lacked maturity context | Marked exploratory concepts and potential applications |
| Ingredient labels gave visitors little reason to investigate | Five question-led home entries linked to existing program panels |
| Oversized repeated icon boxes and inconsistent Korean hierarchy | Compact program figure, responsive headings and Korean hierarchy |
| Legacy lifestyle pages remained in sitemap | Permanent redirects: business to company, products to programs, partnership to contact |
| English-first pages had Korean sharing metadata | Aligned default Open Graph descriptions and locale |
| Scientific context lacked onward reading | Three external reviews clearly distinguished from BIOTRIX results |
| Escape moved focus even with menu closed | Focus restoration only when dismissing an open menu |

## Benchmarks

- https://www.roche.com/ connects patient purpose with solutions and innovation.
- https://www.novartis.com/ separates audience needs, research platforms and pipeline.
- https://www.recursion.com/ connects the AI narrative with platform, process and pipeline.
- Adopt clarity, evidence hierarchy and useful navigation. Visual scale cannot substitute for research results, personnel, facilities or clinical assets.

## Verification

7 pages × 4 widths (320, 390, 768, 1440) × 2 languages: 56 rendering checks passed. No document overflow, failed decoded images, missing/duplicate H1 or research-row overlaps. No browser JavaScript exceptions. Five program panels, mobile menu, Escape, language persistence and three configured redirects passed locally. JavaScript syntax passed. Full-page desktop English home, mobile Korean home and Programs screenshots inspected. Existing logo, hero, approved icons, five concept names and English default retained.

This is Edge/Chromium verification. Safari/iOS, email delivery, accessibility certification, Lighthouse scoring and independent user testing were not performed. Deployment status is recorded in QA_LOG.md and GitHub.

## Remaining gaps

Credibility needs real evidence: a prioritized research problem, documented experiments, identified partners and milestones. Five broad areas still require strategic prioritization. Visitor inspiration is an editorial judgment, not a measured satisfaction outcome. No company-owned IP, clinical efficacy, laboratory capability or available product was established by this review.
